Privacy policy

How Golden Roots Global LLC handles information when you visit the Chatzidis website or contact us.

Updated September 30, 2026

Who we are

Chatzidis is an olive oil brand of Golden Roots Global LLC, 2380 Drew St, Suite 2, Clearwater, FL 33765, USA. For privacy questions or requests, email golden.roots.global.llc@gmail.com or call +1 (786) 645-3841.

Information you choose to share

If you contact us by email or telephone, we receive the details you provide, such as your name, contact information, business details and enquiry. We use this information to respond and discuss your request or order. Please do not send payment-card details or other sensitive information by email.

This website has no customer accounts, online checkout or payment collection. Email links open your own email application; an email is sent only when you send it. Email providers process messages under their own terms and privacy policies.

Hosting and technical information

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes and processes technical information under its privacy statement. Images, fonts and site files are served from this website. We do not load third-party font services.

Cookies and optional analytics

A necessary chatzidis_consent cookie remembers a saved privacy choice for up to 180 days. It stores the choice and configuration version, not your name or contact details. You can remove it using your browser’s cookie controls.

Google Analytics 4 is available only if you allow it. No Google Analytics script loads until you opt in. Declining leaves the whole website available. This site does not load advertising trackers.

When you allow analytics, Google Analytics 4 receives page-visit and contact-link interaction data, along with technical information such as browser and device details. Our page-view events omit query strings and URL fragments; our contact events identify only email or phone, not message contents. Google may process data on servers outside your country. See Google’s privacy policy.

Analytics cookies, including _ga and a property-specific _ga_… cookie, distinguish browser visits. We request a maximum cookie lifetime of 180 days. You may decline analytics or withdraw permission using Cookie settings in the footer. Withdrawing permission stops this site’s future analytics collection and removes its accessible analytics cookies; it does not automatically erase information already received by Google.

Use, sharing and retention

Enquiry information is used to communicate with you, handle requests and maintain relevant business records. Correspondence may be processed by our email and communications providers, or disclosed where required by law. Retention depends on the purpose of the correspondence and applicable recordkeeping requirements. Contact us to ask about information we hold or request deletion.

Your choices

You can use the site without optional analytics, decline to send an enquiry, manage cookies in your browser, or contact us about your personal information. Depending on the laws that apply to you, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict use of your information, and to complain to a relevant privacy authority. We may need to verify your identity before responding to a request.

Links and sharing

Links to other sites are governed by those sites’ privacy practices. The Share button uses your device’s sharing feature or copies the public homepage link. Social-media tracking widgets are not embedded here.

Updates

We may update this policy when the site or its services change. The date above identifies the latest revision. A change to optional analytics configuration requires a new consent choice.
